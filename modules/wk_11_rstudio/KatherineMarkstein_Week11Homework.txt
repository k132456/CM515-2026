  B:
    The class of super_vector is character - 
    this happens because you cannot convert letters (characters) into numbers/class numeric,
    but you can convert numbers into characters/strings.
    
    To force super_vector into a numeric sub-class: super_vector_num <- as.numeric(super_vector)
    Running this code gives the warning message "NAs introduced by coercion".
    Because letter characters cannot be converted to numeric objects, the program could
    only provide NA in the list where these characters existed instead of converting them.
